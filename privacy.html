Privacy Policy

Paddle from Scratch (Padl). Effective November 12, 2026

In short The app collects no personal data. Everything you record stays on your iPhone. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

What the app stores on your device

Saved matches and their scores, lesson progress, your mini-game best score, tournament input and settings such as language. This data stays in the app’s storage on your iPhone. It may be included in your iPhone backup if you have backups turned on.

Purchase

Padl Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Apple. We never receive your payment details; the app only learns from the App Store whether Pro has been bought.

What we do not do

We do not collect, sell or share personal data. The app has no third-party SDKs, ads or analytics and makes no network requests of its own. It does not use the camera, microphone, Location or Health data.

This website

This site has no cookies, analytics or forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare, which process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the pages. If you email us, we use your address only to reply.

Deleting your data

You can delete any saved match in the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from the device.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data about anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
