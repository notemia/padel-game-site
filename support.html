I’ve never played padel. Where do I start?

Open Home → “New to padel? Start here”: nine short lessons on the rules, the court, scoring, the serve, the walls and where to stand.

How do I play the 3D match?

Home → “Play a 3D match”. Drag on the screen to aim: an arc shows the flight. Release to hit. Your partner and the rivals play on their own.

What is free and what is Padl Pro?

Free: the lessons, scoreboard, tournaments, all 13 shots in 3D, Court Lab, three classic rallies and one trial game of the 3D match. Padl Pro is a one-time purchase with no subscription: the full 3D match, every classic rally, match insights and kits.

How do I restore my purchase?

On an iPhone with the same Apple Account, open Settings → Padl Pro → Restore purchases.

How does the scoreboard work?

Score → Start match. Tap a team’s half to add a point. Deuce, golden point, Star Point and tie-breaks are counted for you.

Do I need the internet?

No. Everything is built into the app, and no account is needed.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Language: system default or one of 14 languages. It applies immediately.

Does the app replace a coach?

No. It is a learning aid and a simplified simulation. Learn new shots on court with a coach. The full terms are on the Terms of use page.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 18 or later.
