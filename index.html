Padel from ScratchKnow what to do before your first match

Play a 3D doubles match on a glass court, watch 13 shots in slow motion and learn the rules and scoring in 15 minutes.

Tell me when it’s out

Padel looks like tennis until the ball hits the glass

The court is closed in by glass and mesh, and the ball stays in play after it comes off them. That confuses first-timers most: when is the ball still good, and where do you stand to meet it? In the app you watch it happen in 3D, from any side and at any speed.

In the match you choose every shot yourself: swipe to aim, see the flight arc and release at the right moment. Your partner and the rivals play on their own, and the score follows the official rules, golden point included.

Who it’s for

First-timers
Invited to a game and never held a racket? Nine short lessons cover the rules, the court, scoring, the serve and where to stand.
Club players
See the bandeja, víbora, chiquita and ten more shots from any side in slow motion, and study classic rallies step by step.
Game nights
Keep score with one tap per point and run an Americana or Mexicano tournament with live standings.

What’s inside

Everything works offline, with no account and no ads. The lessons, scoreboard, tournaments, all 13 shots and Court Lab are free.

The 13 shots: Serve, Volley, Bandeja, Globo (lob), Víbora, Chiquita, Smash (remate), Bajada (attack off the glass), Forehand drive (derecha), Backhand (revés), Off the back glass (salida de pared), Drop shot (dejada), Smash out of court (por tres)

A learning aid and a simplified simulation. It does not replace a coach. Not affiliated with the International Padel Federation (FIP).
